# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Give specific, evidence-based feedback
and allow disagreement without personal attacks. Harassment, discriminatory
remarks, threats, doxxing, impersonation and publication of private information
are unacceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit/remove content, lock conversations, limit participation or
block accounts in proportion to the behavior and its context. Enforcement should
explain the relevant behavior when practical while protecting private reports.

Use GitHub's private Report content/Report abuse mechanisms for harassment,
threats or platform abuse; see [reporting abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not repost private or harmful details to make a public report. Suspected
software vulnerabilities follow SECURITY.md instead. GitHub handles platform
reports under its policies; maintainers moderate the project surfaces they control.
